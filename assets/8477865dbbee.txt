Demo update: new ultimate close-ups, and battles can shrink to a mini window
发布日期：2026-11-05 11:12（北京时间）
原文：https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4855890/view/704410099752371038?l=english

Hi everyone, Juanmao here.

The free Demo has been updated again, with new builds for both Windows and Mac. No big new systems this time. I mostly fixed the things you've pointed out over the last few days, and swapped in a batch of new visuals while I was at it.

[h2]Looking punchier[/h2]
[list]
[*]The ultimate close-up has a new look: the battlefield splits diagonally, the hero squeezes out through the middle, and when it's done the whole frame gets swept away.
[*]The protagonist's stance and attack animation have been redrawn. They now stand side-on, facing the enemy.
[*]The Fortress home screen now looks like a map, and the Hall has been renamed the "Agency Hall".
[*]Those little star markers above characters' heads are gone, and when a shield gets knocked out, you'll hear a shield-break sound.
[/list]

[h2]Easier to leave running[/h2]
[list]
[*]There's a new shrink button in the top-right of the battle screen. One click shrinks the battle into a corner mini window and it keeps going, even when you switch to other screens.
[*]Opening the Battle Log while auto-battling no longer pauses the fight.
[*]When a lot of gear drops at once, the notices are merged into one card instead of popping up one after another, and gear from offline stage pushing gets a summary when you come back.
[*]When you replay stages you've already cleared, enemies only get more HP, not more damage, so going back to farm won't leave you unable to win.
[*]Once you've clicked "Add to Wishlist", the game won't remind you again.
[/list]


[h2]Recruiting and heroes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Tavern opens from stage 1, and heroes of 4 stars and below can be recruited cheaply with Legend Recruit Coupons.
[*]3-star and 4-star heroes now cost less to deploy.
[*]A batch of Tank, Cleric, Druid, Bard and Banneret heroes have had their skills reworked. The numbers are still being tuned.
[*]In battle, hover over a buff or debuff to see who gave it.
[*]Formation sorting is now a dropdown, and you can pick "Deployed" to put deployed heroes first.
[/list]
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/0df35c3c2c2543a37d5a850e2e6b990056814407.jpg[/img]

[h2]Other stuff[/h2]
[list]
[*]The prologue dialogue got a new pass, and the part in Chapter 5 where the team falls out has been rewritten.
[*]The tutorial lost a few unnecessary buttons, and the tutorial getting stuck at stage 14 is fixed.
[*]Idling on the Fortress screen takes less from your computer now, and I've smoothed out the stutter when you first open the settings or fire your first ultimate.
[/list]

The save format hasn't changed, so your save should carry over. If it doesn't load or your progress looks off, please let me know.

Let me know in the comments how these changes feel, and whether anything still seems off.

If you think it's not bad, I'd really appreciate it if you added the game to your wishlist. Thank you all. It genuinely matters for a game.
