[h2][color=yellow]What changed in combat and idling[/color][/h2]

Signature abilities darkened the screen and popped up a skill-name banner - [color=lightgreen]The screen stays bright; instead, ink spreads under the hero's feet and specks of light float up[/color]
The close-up for Legendary 5-star ults is still there.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/341b3fe4ee142ca8406149b74b849fef1d4410b7.png[/img]

Ult animations often got skipped - [color=lightgreen]Each of your heroes' first ult in every battle always plays[/color]
The “Gameplay” tab in Settings now has “Skill animations”: pick “On”, “Once per battle” or “Off”.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/4242e4cf98cb1b6f0c3364683f891d9c3b546b01.png[/img]

Loot was only collected after every drop had landed - [color=lightgreen]Loot is collected mid-fight: each piece pauses after landing, then flies into the chest one by one[/color]
Legendary drops get a golden glow, a pillar of light and a nameplate.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/2690fb0aa97325cb1e0ad24a56fd1470978dc677.gif[/img]

Hovering only showed buffs, debuffs and statuses - [color=lightgreen]Click a health bar to pin a card: HP, Shield, Morale, and how much more until their Signature ability[/color]
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/52899f6255d1889f3c7dffad20e29f52bcd93f19.png[/img]

Even a short time offline meant going through the whole debrief - [color=lightgreen]Away under half an hour with only resources? Just a slip of paper slides out. Away longer, the opening scene goes straight into the debrief[/color]
In the debrief, big numbers are shortened (K, M, B), a full day or more is shown in days, and anything over the cap is spelled out too.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/f09d96b0b2d998b5c4b054aff67fb29a36c5008f.gif[/img]
