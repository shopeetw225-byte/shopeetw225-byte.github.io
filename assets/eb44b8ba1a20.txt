Demo v3 arrives October 20, with stages up to 800
发布日期：2026-10-17 18:30（北京时间）
原文：https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4855890/view/760703279879422585?l=english

Hi everyone, Juanmao here.

[b]Demo v3 is coming on October 20.[/b] The last version stopped at stage 200. This time you can keep going all the way to [b]stage 800[/b], with plenty more room to build your team.

Here is what I have been working on.

[h2]Some new faces at the Agency[/h2]
You can recruit 14 new heroes in this Demo. Some drop during the campaign; the other seven are five-star heroes, recruited at the Tavern with their tokens and a Legendary Recruitment Ticket.

Five-star heroes also get a close-up when they use their ultimate. After all that training, they finally get a moment in the spotlight.

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/67f107fd64170c6aaa9a3a980f496718eea7142d.jpg[/img]

[h2]Clear stage 200 and meet your first Guardian Spirit[/h2]
There are 12 to collect. A Spirit can share a formation tile with a hero and grant an aura to nearby allies. When a hero of its affinity class uses a signature skill within range, it can trigger a Resonance effect too.

So now, once you have found a spot for every hero, you have a few little companions to make room for.

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/5a85bc5085db0bdc5b04cd1752bdbcc93988ffaa.jpg[/img]

[h2]Your team keeps pushing while you are away[/h2]
Coming back used to be mostly about collecting resources. Now your team can advance through stages offline, stopping when it loses. The base offline limit is also going from 8 to 12 hours.

That stage you were stuck on before bed? Your team might have a surprise for you in the morning.

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/b25ccb74813183fc4044afce996562580908c8aa.jpg[/img]

[h2]I rewrote the skill descriptions[/h2]
Who does it hit? Who does it help? How far does it reach? The descriptions now spell that out, with diagrams showing the affected tiles. Less reading a paragraph and then guessing where the skill actually lands.

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/de91f7f4e021d0728d7ddd334bfb37e467d6f095.jpg[/img]

[h2]A few more changes you will see and hear[/h2]
Buttons, pop-ups, battle sprites and battlefields have been refreshed, along with the sound effects and music.

You can also improve the whole team’s Mastery in one go, and retrain your Commander for free if you want to try a different team setup.

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/9c2b29944ecfd482c4fcb0a246ec7098ee333698.jpg[/img]

Come back and give it a try after the update on October 20. If you get stuck, find something awkward, or run into a problem, let me know. A screenshot helps me track it down too.
