New players now go straight into Stage 1. The prologue becomes in-battle subtitles, and naming comes later. This part is still under construction, so it is not the finished version yet.

Defeat guidance has changed too: advancement is folded into the upgrade step, and each step appears at most once per defeat. Repeated and skipped prompts are fixed, but I have not finished validating this part yet.

A few more things: group upgrades and advancement need one preview and one confirmation. Recommended lineups keep damage dealers and a healer. The map separates missing heroes from heroes obtainable through idling. Explicit challenges go to the front line instead of sending you back to farm. The demo has a wrap-up page for results, wishlisting, or playing on. The download is smaller too, with static portraits in the demo.
