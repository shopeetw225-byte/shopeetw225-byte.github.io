Hi everyone, I'm Juanmao, the developer of an indie game called Idle Agency.

[b]The battles run automatically. You figure out the formation.[/b] Arrange your heroes on a 4×4 grid and let them fight. Progress continues while you're offline, so you can return to a pile of rewards. There are 146 heroes to collect without gacha. It's a buy-to-play game with no stamina system and no daily check-in pressure.

[b]A free demo will be available during Steam Next Fest[/b], and I'll keep working on the game. There's still plenty to improve, so please tell me what isn't fun—I'll be reading your feedback.

If this sounds like your kind of game, please [b]wishlist and follow[/b] Idle Agency. Thank you!
