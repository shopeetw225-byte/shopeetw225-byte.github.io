```text
[b]新增[/b]

[list]
[*]新玩家开局直接进入第 1 关战斗，序章改为战斗中的字幕，取名延后；情境说明卡最多只会再弹出一次。（仍在施工中）

[*]开场战胜利后的主按钮改为“返回要塞”，三种语言均已更新；改名提示也已配合当前流程调整。
[*]战败引导中的全队升阶并入全队升级步骤，不再分成两步。

[*]试玩版 Windows 安装包移除最早六段立绘动画（约 60 MB），试玩版立绘统一改为静态。
[*]正式出货的大图统一采用有损压缩，以缩小游戏容量。

[list]

[*]修复推荐上阵在预算紧张时可能剔除治疗英雄的问题，现在会保留治疗英雄。

[*]征途地图区间列的缺员文字改为两行显示，避免挤压挂机按钮。
[*]“挂机可得”现已计入传说招募信物，酒馆招募也补充了说明提示。
[*]修复主动发起征途挑战或在编队页按 GO 时，可能被拉回重刷前一关的问题。主动挑战现在会立即进入最前线；只有自动恢复时，才会在升级后或 10 分钟后接回重刷。
[*]突破材料改用印章勾选图标；修复 BOSS 页底部说明的换行，并将战力参考框移至数值上方。
[*]修正全队升级时成长音效的写法，避免被音效检查误判。
[/list]
```

## English

标题：October 8 Update Notes

```text
[b]What's New[/b]

[list]
[*]New players now start directly in the Stage 1 battle. The prologue plays as in-battle subtitles, naming comes later, and the context card will appear at most once more. (Still in development.)
[*]Added step-by-step guidance after early defeats to help new players strengthen their team, along with a guaranteed deployment budget for Stage 4 and detection of stalled progress. (Pending validation.)
[*]Added a demo wrap-up screen with a thank-you message, your results, a wishlist link, save transfer information, and an option to keep playing.
[/list]

[b]Changes[/b]

[list]
[*]The full game will no longer load demo saves. The demo wrap-up screen now makes it clear that you will need to start fresh in the full game.
[*]The main button after winning the opening battle now reads "Return to Fortress" in all three languages. The rename prompt has also been updated to match the current flow.
[*]Team-wide rank-ups are now included in the team-wide level-up step of the defeat guide, instead of being a separate step.
[*]Team-wide level-up and rank-up popups now let you confirm after a single preview. They close automatically on success and stay open with an explanation on failure.
[*]The lineup hint for activating a synergy with one more hero now appears only when an eligible hero of the same class is on the bench and you can afford to deploy them.
[*]Campaign map section rows now show the number of missing heroes separately from the number available through idle rewards.
[*]The catch-up discount now applies only when a hero is at least 2 ranks below the median.
[*]Replaced 15 approved battlefield icons with final artwork. The remaining icons still use programmatically drawn graphics for now.
[*]Removed the six earliest animated portraits (about 60 MB) from the Windows demo package. All portraits in the demo are now static.
[*]Large images in shipped builds now use lossy compression to reduce the game's size.
[/list]

[b]Fixes[/b]

[list]
[*]Fixed recommended lineups at budgets of 8/12 sometimes containing only sustain heroes and no damage dealers. Recommendations now reserve a minimum number of damage-dealing slots.
[*]Fixed recommended lineups sometimes dropping the healer when the budget was tight. The healer is now retained.
[*]Fixed defeat-guide steps repeating during the same defeat. Each step now prompts at most once, and progress tracking starts from the victory results screen.
[*]Fixed the power check skipping the defeat guide on Stage 20. Timeout defeats now complete the guide before returning to retry; one of the prompts appears only for timeout defeats.
[*]Moved opening-battle subtitles to the bottom of the battlefield so they no longer cover units' faces. Subtitles now use a single line and their own timer to prevent loading stalls from skipping them.
[*]Missing-hero text in campaign map section rows now uses two lines so it no longer crowds the idle button.
[*]Idle reward estimates now include legendary recruitment tokens. Added an explanatory tooltip to tavern recruitment as well.
[*]Fixed manually starting a campaign challenge or pressing GO on the lineup screen sometimes sending you back to farm the previous stage. Manual challenges now take you straight to the frontline. Only automatic resumption returns to farming after a level-up or 10 minutes.
[*]Breakthrough materials now use a stamp-and-checkmark icon. Fixed text wrapping at the bottom of the BOSS page and moved the power reference box above the value.
[*]Corrected the growth sound-effect definition used during team-wide level-ups to prevent a false positive in audio validation.
[/list]
```
