October 5 Update | Sorting Out Who Gives Shields
发布日期：2026-10-05 10:10（北京时间）
原文：https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4855890/view/687518426922485695?l=english

This time I sorted out the support roles. Shields now come from the appropriate classes, and I went through the physical and magical damage paths too. Shield-related equipment is still useful, so your collection has not gone to waste.

A few awkward display issues are fixed as well: percentage bonuses no longer appear twice. The formation screen labels allied positions, and aura descriptions tell you which team and how many rows they affect. Less guesswork.

Two combat fixes too: the Bard’s morale now affects only allies, and defensive-banner auras no longer make an entire row’s shields disappear.
