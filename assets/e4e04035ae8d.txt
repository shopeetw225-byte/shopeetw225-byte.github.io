October 2 Update | A New Look for the Academy
发布日期：2026-10-02 13:16（北京时间）
原文：https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4855890/view/687518426922484734?l=english

The Academy got most of the attention this time. The overview circle, four skill-tree branches, info cards, era tabs, and total bonuses all have new designs and animations.

Drag and zoom the skill tree, select a node to focus on it, and clear the selection to return. Want your total bonuses? Just click the sign. There is an ink-wash entrance effect, gold connections between learned nodes, and research connections that light up as progress advances.

All twelve classes now have a class trait that actually affects combat, and Mastery 6 advancement is available too. The exclusive weapon stamp progression and advancement effects are still in development. The image below is an interface overview, so keep that in mind.
