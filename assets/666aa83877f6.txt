Demo update: reworked prologue, improved battle visuals and loading
发布日期：2026-10-24 10:22（北京时间）
原文：https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4855890/view/704408827984877238?l=english

Hi everyone, Juanmao here.

The Demo has another update, and it's free to download on Steam for both Windows and macOS. This one mainly covers three things: the prologue, the battle visuals, and stutter.

[h2]Prologue: two fights, no choices[/h2]
The prologue for new saves no longer has any choices. You go straight into two battles. In the first, the screen pauses for a beat when someone fires an ultimate, and enemies flash a warning before they cast a skill, so it's easier to see who's about to act.
The second battle... well, the boss wipes out the whole party in one move, and the five legends are scattered. Only after that fight does the tutorial properly begin.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/fd6a5c4e8815af1e7523c099ce3bb1d0b18a9739.jpg[/img]

[h2]Battle visuals: easier to read[/h2]
HP and morale bars now use a paper-tag style. You'll get a cue when a shield is about to break, and you can see it shatter. The Paladin now gets up close and bashes enemies with a shield. Status effects have been redone, characters have a soft shadow at their feet, and hover tooltips now look like parchment.
[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46216320/ea2ffdfe2d7980b3b9235902b831541cfb6224be.jpg[/img]

[h2]Less stutter[/h2]
This update reduces stutter when loading after a long time offline, in late-game battles, and when you continue your game.

[h2]Fixed[/h2]
[list]
[*]Completing a Legendary Seal showed no feedback.
[*]Quality badges in the socket list overlapped names.
[*]An extra sound effect occasionally played.
[*]Boss battle HP bars sometimes failed to appear.
[/list]

If anything feels off while you play, let me know in the comments. A screenshot helps me track it down.
