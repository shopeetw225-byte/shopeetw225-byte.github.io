First up: idling. When choosing a chapter, you can now preview its materials, sets, and legendary drops. Quantities increase with campaign progress. Targeted commissions also have badges for “already owned” and “not dropped here,” so you know what you are farming for.

Then there are the click-savers: Academy research unlocks automatic Market trades for base materials and purchases of the day’s recruitment tickets. Free research rushes are used automatically. Break through runes in one click with a preview of remaining stones, open all chest tickets at once, and auto-dismantle up to Epic. Offline reports also show losses from full storage.

Combat now has heavy-hit pauses, skill-hit markers, and original fantasy music and sound effects.
